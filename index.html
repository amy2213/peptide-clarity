Verify It · Learn the limits

What does a lab certificate actually show?

A certificate may report chemical identity, purity, content or particular contaminants for an analyzed sample. The document alone doesn't prove a sold product is the tested sample.

Purity

What proportion of detected material a particular analytical method attributes to the target under its measurement conditions.

What does this mean?
A reported 99% chromatographic area purity isn't the same as 99% of the labeled milligrams, sterility, or medical safety.

Content / assay

How much compound was measured in the analyzed sample.

What does this mean?
Compare the same compound and units on the label and report. A quantity difference doesn't imply better clinical results.

Endotoxins

Measurement of certain bacterial components using a specific test and units.

What does this mean?
An endotoxin result isn't an all-purpose contamination or sterility clearance. Interpretation depends on method and applicable limits.

Sterility

A distinct test question about viable microorganisms under a validated procedure.

What does this mean?
A generic bacterial count or PCR screen cannot automatically be relabeled as validated sterility testing. If no method/result is shown, mark it unknown.

Transparent methods

Evidence, not endorsements.

The three separate questions

  • Science: What do clinical and other studies establish about a substance?
  • Supplier documentation: What do published records report about a particular product or lot?
  • Our review: Which exact records and facts have we actually inspected or authenticated?

Document review levels

  • L0: An index or claim observed, not the underlying report.
  • L1: Underlying PDF examined on supplier site.
  • L2: A matching issuer-domain document examined, but issuance not independently attested.
  • L3–L5: Would require issuer confirmation, sample custody and qualified analysis. None achieved in this prototype.

Unknown isn't passed or failed

We don't turn absent assay records into green check marks or accuse companies of wrongdoing for something we haven't checked.

Human review still matters

The profiles are owner-led desk research drafts. Independent analytical, scientific, legal and accessibility review is outstanding. Public launch has not been approved.
