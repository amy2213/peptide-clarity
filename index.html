Critical distinction

What an independent reviewer would need

  1. Issuer: evidence laboratory really issued the report.
  2. Source: who collected or purchased the physical sample?
  3. Lot: document and product identifiers reconcile.
  4. Analytical scope: what tests, methods, findings and exclusions exist?
  5. Contradictions: identify discrepancies and unresolved claims.
  6. Review decision: qualified reviewer, timestamp and evidence record.
