# Peptide Clarity v0.7 | Eight plain-English source-linked profiles

Working **owner-testing** draft based on v0.6.1. Preserves search, browse, COA education and company/lot comparisons while adding eight beginner-friendly summaries. No supplier certification, drug prescribing, dose or purchasing assistance. Published research and FDA data are attributed, but qualified editorial/expert review is pending.

- `index.html`: open in browser (self-contained static app)
- `data.json`: inspectable source, science, company and lot records
- `FEATURE-008_SOURCE_LINKED_PROFILES.md`: product and source notes
- `QA-008_PROFILE_EXPANSION.md`: automated check results and limitations

Do not merge into public `main` without a separately documented release decision and required reviews.
