# Peptide Clarity UX-010 | Dark + 3D visual system v0.9

**State:** Owner-led **design review**, not a public product release or qualified scientific review.  
**Built:** November 8, 2026.  
**Previous working build:** v0.8 (`main`); evidence ledger remains the v0.7 source snapshot.

## Problem / owner direction
The owner prefers the premium black background, luminous teal accents, and 3D molecular visuals shown in the approved direction mockup. A mockup is not a functioning website. This iteration translates the concept into a real static site while preserving the original plain-English education and evidence transparency goals.

## What changed
- Near-black background, high-contrast white typography, luminous teal accents, subtle glass panels, and integrated original decorative 3D biotech art.
- The 3D art is **generic illustration**, not a chemical structure, microscope image, test result, or representation of a particular peptide. It is intentionally decorative and should not imply scientific authenticity.
- A search-first hero with the owner-approved clarity positioning. One prominent peptide search, then three clear entryways: peptide education, company evidence, and COA interpretation.
- Dark styling propagated through peptide profiles, source links, company dossiers, three-company comparisons, COA guide, badges, dropdowns, and iPhone bottom navigation.
- Two optimized WebP art backgrounds are embedded as CSS data URLs in the single-page HTML. No external host is needed for the images; all content is visible on static GitHub Pages.
- Added visible `DESIGN PREVIEW v0.9` labeling; retained `noindex,nofollow,noarchive` and the footer limitations.

## What did not change
- No rewrite of substance science, approval status, claims, source links, company data, laboratory/lot findings, issuer-authentication state, custody claims, or evidence issues.
- The embedded `researchData` JSON matches v0.8 **byte for byte**.
- 84 searchable terms; 13 source-linked draft research profiles; 41 evidence-source records; five supplier dossiers; fifteen company/substance/lot associations; five unresolved research issues.
- No dosing, medical advice, endorsements, transactions, COA upload/authentication, artificial product safety scores, or unreviewed AI conclusions.

## Evaluation pathway
1. Open the v0.9 GitHub Pages preview directly in Safari on iPhone.
2. Search `Selank` or `BPC-157` from home, open a profile and inspect what the evidence actually says.
3. Use the tab bar to open Compare, inspect a supplier dossier, then open COA guide and choose Sterility.
4. Check clarity, navigation, readability, spacing, performance on the actual device, and how 3D art affects understanding.
