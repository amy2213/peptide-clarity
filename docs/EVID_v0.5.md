## Source-verification ladder

- **L0 =** Vendor or third-party index/page personally observed; underlying issued report not inspected.
- **L1 =** Supplier-hosted purported laboratory PDF personally inspected; issuer did not independently attest authenticity, sample origin or custody.
- **L2 =** Supplier PDF and issuer-domain PDF found to correspond in an earlier documented desk review. Does not establish sample procurement, authenticity confirmed by laboratory personnel, proper test method, or sold-lot identity. A current attempt to retrieve the issuer copy failed.
- **L3 =** Independent issuer attestation via validated portal or official contact. **Not achieved.**
- **L4 =** Documented independent sample procurement and custody. **Not achieved.**
- **L5 =** Qualified analytical methods review. **Not achieved.**

These labels describe *depth of document review*, not a health-risk score, quality rating, company ranking, or pass/fail outcome.

## Direct PDF inspection: Particle Peptides BPC-157

**Public index:** https://particlepeptides.com/en/coa-vault  
**Seller-hosted purported Liquilabs PDF:** https://particlepeptides.com/modules/be_coa/upload/coa_20260903_203243_569cbeac.pdf  
**Index lot:** PO-2026346; **PDF batch:** 2026346 (mapping not attested); **PDF date:** 2026-09-03.  
**Reported assay/content:** 6.38 mg against product label 5 mg, **27.6% above labeled amount** in the analyzed sample. **Reported purity:** 99.2%.  
**Reported microbial result:** total aerobic count 0 CFU/g and yeast/mold count 0 CFU/g. **Reported endotoxin:** 0.486 EU/mg. These are not a blanket sterility test, an interpreted medical-use limit, or a pass/fail certification.

Method: visually inspected PDF page 1 (issuer branding, sample, batch and date) and page 2 (assay/test panel). **Limit:** the named issuer was not contacted or confirmed; laboratory public lookup not queried; sample-selection process and consumer-product correspondence unknown. A 0 CFU/g result in a sampled test does not guarantee sterile sold products.
